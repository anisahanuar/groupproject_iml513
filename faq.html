Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is HolaGO? +

HolaGO is a fun and easy online platform that helps beginners learn Spanish through simple lessons, quizzes, audio guides, and interactive activities.

2. Who can use HolaGO? +

Anyone can use HolaGO! Whether you are a student, a complete beginner, or just curious about Spanish, our website is designed for all learning levels.

3. Do I need any Spanish background before using HolaGO? +

No! HolaGO is beginner-friendly. You can start from zero and learn step by step at your own pace.

4. Is HolaGO free to use? +

Yes, all basic features such as lessons, quizzes, and pronunciation guides are completely free.

5. What can I learn on HolaGO? +

You can learn Spanish essentials like greetings, numbers, colours, daily phrases, and pronunciation. More topics will be added over time!

6. How do I use the quizzes? +

Just click the PLAY button on any quiz card and you’ll be redirected to a Kahoot challenge where you can test your Spanish skills.

7. Can I access HolaGO on my phone? +

Absolutely! HolaGO is mobile-friendly and works on phones, tablets, and computers.

8. Who created HolaGO? +

HolaGO was created as a student project to help Malaysians learn Spanish in a fun, interactive, and beginner-friendly way.
